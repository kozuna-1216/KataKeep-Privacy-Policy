Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how KataKeep (the “App”) handles information about its users.

1. Information We Store

The App stores information you enter, such as replacement item names, part numbers, purchase URLs, notes, replacement history, reminder settings, and label photos. This data is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud sync, in your iCloud account.

2. Camera and Photos

With your action, the App can access your camera or photo library so you can add label photos. Text recognition is performed on your device using Apple frameworks. Your photos are not sent to an external server for text recognition.

3. iCloud Sync

If iCloud is available and enabled for you, your data is synced to your iCloud account through Apple CloudKit. The scope, retention, and security of this sync are subject to Apple's settings and terms. The App developer does not access or retrieve your iCloud data.

4. Notifications

The App schedules local notifications on your device only when you configure replacement reminders. The App does not send notification content to an external server.

5. Purchases

KataKeep Plus purchases are handled through Apple's StoreKit and the App Store. The App does not collect or store your Apple payment information. The App may access transaction information as needed to check purchase status.

6. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide item, replacement history, reminder, iCloud sync, and KataKeep Plus features. The App does not collect or sell user data for advertising.

7. Disclosure to Third Parties

The App does not disclose your data to third parties unless you consent, disclosure is required by law, or Apple services are used to provide iCloud sync.

8. Deleting Your Data

You can delete data stored on your device using the App's item deletion feature or by uninstalling the App. If iCloud sync is enabled, deleting an item in the App also deletes its synced data. Complete deletion of data stored in iCloud is also subject to your Apple account settings.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when necessary. We will provide notice of material changes on this page or in the App.
